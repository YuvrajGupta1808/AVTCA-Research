\opening{Dear Editor,}

We submit the manuscript ``AVB-Engage: Audio-Visual and Facial-Behavior Fusion for Ordinal Student Engagement
Recognition'' for consideration as an original research article in the \emph{Journal of Ambient Intelligence and
Humanized Computing}.

The paper addresses automatic recognition of student engagement in online learning from a webcam recording. It
combines an audio-visual cross-attention network with two models on facial-behavior statistics and decodes the four
ordered engagement levels through the expected level. On the official test split of the EngageNet dataset it reaches
70.29\% top-1 accuracy over three training seeds, against 67.61\% for the best published result on that split, and it
measures on the same clips how much of the gain comes from the decoding rule, from combining models that make different
errors, and from the audio track.

The manuscript follows the journal's instructions: it is prepared with the Springer Nature \LaTeX{} template in the
two-column layout, is 12 pages long including the references, and contains the Statements and Declarations. Supplementary material is provided as Online Resource~1 (ESM\_1.pdf). Figures~1 and~2 were drawn with
draw.io, with the data panels of Figures~1 and~2 computed from the dataset and our result files, and Figure~3 with TikZ in \LaTeX; all are
supplied as EPS files. Figures~1 and~2 show a drawn face, not a participant.

This work is original, has not been published elsewhere, and is not under consideration by another journal; a
preprint of the manuscript is publicly available, with the code, in the GitHub repository given in its Code
availability statement. All
authors have approved the manuscript and its submission. The manuscript contains no previously published material
that requires permission. The use of generative AI tools is described in the Methods and in the Statements and
Declarations. [Optional: we suggest the following independent reviewers: name, institution, institutional e-mail.]
